# Competition rules

This is a private INFO 511 course competition. Course academic-integrity policies and these competition-specific rules apply.

1. Use only the competition files, course-provided materials, and generally available software libraries. External data are not allowed unless the instructor announces an exception to the entire class.
2. Do not retrieve ozone labels from EPA/AQS or any other public source. Do not attempt to reverse pseudonymous IDs or infer withheld exact site/date keys.
3. Do not seek, use, or share hidden labels, private instructor files, another team's predictions, or code that reproduces another team's submission.
4. Collaboration is limited to your registered Kaggle/course team. Follow the instructor's team-size and collaboration policy.
5. Submit probabilities between 0 and 1. Attempts to exploit the scoring system are prohibited.
6. Keep a reproducible record of preprocessing, validation, models, hyperparameters, random seeds, and submissions.
7. The leaderboard is not the whole assignment. Your report must include honest validation, multiple metrics, error analysis, limitations, and substantive interpretation.
8. Describe `high_ozone` as the course threshold of daily maximum 8-hour ozone above 70 ppb. Do not call an individual positive row an EPA regulatory violation.
9. The instructor may invalidate work that uses prohibited data, exploits leakage, cannot be reproduced, or violates course policy.

There are no cash prizes. Grades are determined by the course rubric, not solely by Kaggle rank.
